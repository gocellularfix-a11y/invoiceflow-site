## Summary

InvoiceFlow keeps your business information on your own device and does not collect it.

- You do not need an account, and we have no servers that hold your data.
- The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- One optional feature uses the internet: online address suggestions. It is **off until you turn it on**, after the app tells you what it sends.
- Anything else that leaves your device does so because you chose to share it, for example opening an invoice in WhatsApp.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores what you enter so that you can create invoices. All of it stays in the app's private storage on your device:

- Your business profile: name, email, phone, address, tax ID, website and logo.
- Your customers: name, email, phone, address (street, city, state and ZIP), preferred language and notes.
- Invoices and estimates: items, prices, quantities, taxes, fees, discounts, notes, dates and numbers.
- Payments you record, receipts, reminders you log, and a log of messages you chose to share.
- Photos and images you attach to an invoice or customer, including photos you take with your camera.
- Your settings, such as language, currency, tax rate, default fees and your choice about address suggestions.

We do not receive any of this information and cannot see it. It never reaches a server of ours. The customer details you enter belong to your business, and you are responsible for handling them under the laws that apply to you.

## Information that leaves your device

Only a few things leave your device, and each one is described here.

**Online address suggestions (optional, off by default).** If you turn this on, the street or city you type in the customer form is sent to Photon (photon.komoot.io), a free address-search service run by komoot that uses OpenStreetMap data. Before it is turned on, the app shows you this explanation and asks you to confirm. The request contains only the text you typed and a few fixed search settings. It carries no cookies and does not say which page of the app you were on. Photon necessarily also receives your device's IP address and the usual technical details of any internet request. We send nothing else: no customer names, invoices, phone numbers or other fields. komoot's general privacy policy is at [komoot.com/privacy](https://www.komoot.com/privacy). It does not specifically describe the Photon service, so we cannot tell you how long komoot keeps these requests. Address data comes from OpenStreetMap contributors and is available under the Open Database License ([openstreetmap.org/copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)). You can turn this off at any time in Settings or under the street field in the customer form. When it is off, no request is made and the app only suggests addresses of customers you already saved.

**Things you choose to share.** When you share an invoice, receipt, reminder, PDF, CSV export or backup, it goes where you send it, for example WhatsApp or email, through your device's share menu or a link. For WhatsApp the app opens a wa.me link with a prepared message, and you decide whether to press send there. The app cannot tell whether you did, so its activity log says a message was "shared", "opened in WhatsApp" or "logged", never that it was delivered. The message and the phone number are handled by WhatsApp under its own terms, and only if you send it. We do not see or store them. The app never sends messages by itself.

**Payment links.** If you add a Stripe Payment Link in Settings, the app accepts only links on Stripe's payment-link address (buy.stripe.com). It attaches your invoice number to that link as a reference, so you can match a payment in your Stripe Dashboard. Customer names and other personal details are not added to the link. InvoiceFlow does not process payments, never handles card details, and cannot see whether a customer paid: you record payments yourself.

**Device backups.** Your phone may back up the app's data to your Google account if you have device backup turned on. This is a feature of your phone and your Google account, and it can include customer details and photos. InvoiceFlow asks Android to upload that backup only when it can be encrypted end to end with your screen lock, and not at all otherwise. We never receive it. You can review or turn off backups in your phone's settings (Settings, then Google services, then Backup).

**Google Play.** Google may share anonymous crash and usage statistics with us through Google Play if you have allowed your device to send them. These do not contain your invoices or customer data.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect your personal information at all, because it never reaches us. Specifically, the app has:

- no accounts, sign-up or login;
- no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools of its own;
- no advertising and no advertising ID;
- no access to your location, contacts or microphone. Address suggestions use only the text you type, not GPS;
- no camera permission of its own. Taking a photo opens your device's camera, and the app receives only the photo you choose to attach.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

## Your choices and control

You control your data directly in the app.

- **Online address suggestions** are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time. Nothing is then sent to Photon.
- **Export a backup** from Settings under Data & Backup. A backup is a plain file that is **not encrypted** and contains your customers' details and photos, so keep it somewhere private.
- **Delete data** as described in the next section.

## Deleting your data

Because the data lives only on your device, you delete it there. We hold no copy to delete.

1. **One record:** open an invoice, customer or attachment and delete it.
2. **Everything in the app:** in Settings, choose Clear all data and confirm. This removes all invoices, customers, payments, reminders, photos, settings and your language choice from the app.
3. **Remove the app:** uninstalling InvoiceFlow removes its data from your device.

Deleting data, clearing it or uninstalling does **not** delete:

- backup files you exported and saved or shared (delete those yourself where you stored them);
- copies kept by your phone's own backup in your Google account (manage them in your phone's backup settings or your Google Account);
- messages or files you shared with other people or apps.

It also does not delete anything that Photon or WhatsApp received from you, which are handled under their own terms.

We keep nothing on our side. The app keeps your data until you delete it.
