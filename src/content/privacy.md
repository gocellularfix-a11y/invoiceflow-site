## Summary

InvoiceFlow keeps your business information on your own device and does not collect it.

- You do not need an account, and we have no servers that hold your data.
- The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- One optional feature uses the internet: address suggestions while you type a customer's address. You can turn it off.
- Anything else that leaves your device does so because you chose to share it, for example a PDF sent through WhatsApp.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores what you enter so that you can create invoices. All of it stays in the app's private storage on your device:

- Your business profile: name, email, phone, address, tax ID, website and logo.
- Your customers: name, email, phone, address, preferred language and notes.
- Invoices and estimates: items, prices, quantities, taxes, fees, discounts, notes, dates and numbers.
- Payments and receipts you record, reminders you create, and a log of messages you chose to send.
- Photos and images you attach to an invoice or customer, including photos you take with your camera.
- Your settings, such as language, currency, tax rate and default fees.

We do not receive any of this information and cannot see it. It never reaches a server of ours. The customer details you enter belong to your business, and you are responsible for handling them under the laws that apply to you.

## Information that leaves your device

Only a few things leave your device, and each one is described here.

**Address suggestions (optional, on by default).** When you type a street or city in the customer form, the text you have typed is sent to Photon (photon.komoot.io), a free address-search service built on OpenStreetMap data and run by komoot. This is how the app suggests a full address. Along with the text, the service necessarily receives your device's IP address, as any internet request does. We send nothing else: no customer names, invoices or other fields. Photon's own privacy policy applies to what it does with the request. You can turn this off under the street field in the customer form. When it is off, no request is made and the app only suggests addresses of customers you already saved.

**Things you choose to share.** When you send an invoice, receipt, reminder, PDF, CSV export or backup, it goes where you send it, for example WhatsApp or email, through your device's share menu or a link. For WhatsApp the app opens a wa.me link with a prefilled message. The message and the phone number are then handled by WhatsApp under its own terms, and only if you press send. We do not see or store them.

**Payment links.** If you add a Stripe Payment Link in Settings, the app attaches your invoice number and the customer's name to that link as a reference, so you can match payments in your Stripe dashboard. That reference is visible to Stripe and to the person who opens the link. InvoiceFlow does not process payments and never handles card details.

**Device backups.** Android may back up app data to your Google account if you have device backup turned on. That is a feature of your phone and your Google account, controlled in your device settings, not something we receive.

**Google Play.** Google may share anonymous crash and usage statistics with us through Google Play if you have allowed your device to send them. These do not contain your invoices or customer data.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect your personal information at all, because it never reaches us. Specifically, the app has:

- no accounts, sign-up or login;
- no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools of its own;
- no advertising and no advertising ID;
- no access to your location, contacts or microphone. Address suggestions use only the text you type, not GPS;
- no camera permission of its own. Taking a photo opens your device's camera, and the app receives only the photo you choose to attach.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

## Your choices and control

You control your data directly in the app.

- **Turn off online suggestions** under the street field in the customer form. Nothing is then sent to Photon.
- **Export a backup** from Settings under Data & Backup. A backup is a plain file that is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.
- **Delete data** by deleting individual invoices, customers or attachments in the app, by using Clear all data in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Because the data lives only on your device, deleting it or uninstalling the app removes it permanently unless you made a backup first.

## Children, security and retention

The app is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which your device's operating system protects. We recommend a screen lock and device encryption. Because we hold no copy, there is no database of ours that could be breached. Files you export or share are outside the app and your responsibility.

We keep nothing on our side. The app keeps your data until you delete it.
