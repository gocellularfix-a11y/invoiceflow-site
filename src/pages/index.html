Your data stays on your device

InvoiceFlow has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We do not have a server that stores your customers or invoices.

  • Customers, invoices and payments are stored only on your phone.
  • The only online feature is optional address suggestions. They are off until you turn them on, and the app tells you what is sent first.
  • Because we keep no copy, you are in charge of backups. Export one from Settings whenever you like.

Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Install the app, enter your business name and start invoicing.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Creating invoices, recording payments and reports all work offline. Only the optional address suggestions and opening a message in WhatsApp need a connection.

Where is my data stored, and how do I back it up?

On your device only. Open Settings, choose Data & Backup and export a backup file. Keep it somewhere safe, and import it to restore. See the support page for the steps.

Does InvoiceFlow take payments?

No. If you use Stripe, you can attach your own Stripe Payment Link and share it with the invoice. Payments go straight between you, your customer and Stripe, and InvoiceFlow cannot see them: you record a payment yourself once you have confirmed it.

Which currencies and languages are supported?

US dollars, Mexican pesos and Brazilian reais, in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

Does the app calculate my taxes for me?

It calculates the tax you set. You choose the name, the rate and which items are taxable, and you are responsible for charging the right amount. InvoiceFlow does not give tax advice.
