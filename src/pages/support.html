Reporting a problem

The fastest way for us to help is to include:

  • what you were doing and what happened instead;
  • your phone model and Android version;
  • a screenshot, with any customer details hidden.

Back up your data

InvoiceFlow keeps everything on your device and we keep no copy, so a backup is the only way to recover your data if you lose or reset your phone.

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Choose Data & Backup, then Export backup.
  3. Save the file somewhere safe, such as your email or cloud drive. It is not encrypted.

Restore from a backup

  1. Install InvoiceFlow on your new device.
  2. Open Settings, then Data & Backup.
  3. Choose the backup file you exported. Restoring replaces the data currently in the app.

Delete your data

Because the data lives only on your device, you delete it there. We hold no copy.

  1. One record: open the invoice, customer or attachment and delete it.
  2. Everything in the app: in Settings, choose Clear all data and confirm.
  3. Remove the app: uninstall InvoiceFlow.

This does not delete backup files you exported and saved or shared, copies kept by your phone's own backup, or messages you sent to other people. To manage your phone's backup, open Settings, then Google services, then Backup. Backup files you exported are not encrypted, so delete them yourself where you stored them.

Common questions

My taxes look wrong. The app uses the tax name, rate and taxable items you set in Settings and on each invoice. Check those first.

Address suggestions do not appear. They are off until you turn them on, under the street field in the customer form or in Settings. They also need an internet connection.

I want to use the app on a second device. There is no sync yet. Export a backup on one device and restore it on the other.
